Who We Are

Building Africa's
Innovation Backbone.

MG Innovation Hub was founded in 2022 by a group of engineers, entrepreneurs, and investors who saw a gap: East Africa had world-class talent but lacked a home for it. So they built one.

Today we operate from Nakuru, Kenya, running incubation programs, workshops, co-working spaces, and investment networks that have collectively helped 18+ startups reach product-market fit, raise capital, and scale across the continent.

Mission

Accelerate Africa's most impactful innovators from idea to scale.

Vision

A continent where every bold idea has a launchpad.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values.

These aren't framed posters on a wall. They're the operating principles that shape every program, every partnership, and every conversation.

⚡

Audacious Ambition

We don't celebrate incremental. We back founders who want to redefine industries, not just optimise them. Big swings are the norm here.

🤝

Community First

The hub is built on reciprocity. Every member contributes knowledge, connections, and energy — and receives the same in return. Isolation ends at our doors.

🌍

Africa-First Lens

Every solution we build must make sense in context. We design for African infrastructure, African users, and African opportunity — not imported frameworks.

🔬

Evidence Over Hype

We ask hard questions. We demand data. Enthusiasm is welcome but it doesn't replace rigorous testing, user research, and honest validation.

🔓

Radical Transparency

With our founders, our partners, and our community — we say what we mean. Clear feedback, honest guidance, and no hidden agendas.

🚀

Execution Wins

Ideas are cheap. Velocity is rare. We celebrate founders who ship fast, learn fast, and iterate relentlessly — momentum over perfection, always.

How We Got Here

Our Journey.

2022
Launch Of MG Hub

Partnership agreements signed with hubs in Kampala, Dar es Salaam, and Kigali. MG becomes the anchor node in a cross-border innovation network spanning East Africa.

2023
Corporate Innovation Division

Launch of the Corporate Innovation service line. Within 12 months, partnerships are signed with 6 enterprise clients including a top-5 Kenyan bank and two telcos.

2024
The $0.5M Milestone

Cumulative funding facilitated for Hub alumni surpasses $0.5M. The 18th startup crosses the incubation finish line. A new 3-floor HQ is inaugurated in Upperhill.

2025 →
The Next Chapter

Launching MG Labs, a deep-tech R&D arm, and MG Fund — a $5M seed vehicle exclusively for Hub alumni. Pan-African ambitions, continental footprint.
