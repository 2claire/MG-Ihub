About MG

We Build the Infrastructure
for Bold Ideas.

MG Innovation Hub was founded on a single conviction: that Africa's most transformative breakthroughs are still ahead. We provide the tools, mentorship, workspace, and capital connections to turn that potential into reality.

Based in Nakuru and expanding across East Africa, we sit at the intersection of technology, entrepreneurship, and community — creating an environment where creativity is never constrained.
