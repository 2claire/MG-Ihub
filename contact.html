Before You Write

Common Questions.

Where exactly is MG Innovation Hub in Nakuru?
+
We're located on the 5th Floor, Riva Plaza, Kenyatta Avenue in Nakuru CBD — right in the heart of the city. The building is directly opposite CK Patel Building. It's well signposted and easy to find.
Can I visit the hub without an appointment?
+
Yes — drop-ins are welcome during office hours (Mon–Fri 8 AM–6 PM, Sat 9 AM–2 PM). Just sign in at the front desk. If you'd like a tour or a conversation with our team, booking ahead ensures we can give you proper time and attention.
How quickly will you respond to my message?
+
We guarantee a response within 48 business hours. In practice, most enquiries receive a reply within 4–8 hours during working days. For urgent matters, calling or WhatsApp messaging is the fastest route.
Do you have a Nairobi or other branch?
+
Our headquarters is in Nakuru. We have active partnership agreements with hubs in Nairobi, Kampala, Dar es Salaam, and Kigali — so if you're in those cities, we can connect you with the right space. A Nairobi satellite office is planned for Q3 2026.
I'm a journalist or researcher — who should I contact?
+
Email our press team directly at press@mginnovationhub.co. Please include your publication name, the nature of your enquiry, and your deadline. We typically respond to press enquiries within 24 hours.
Can I host my own event or workshop at your space?
+
Absolutely. Our event spaces are available for hire — including the 300-seat auditorium, workshop labs, and the rooftop terrace. Use the enquiry form above (select "Events & Partnerships") or email events@mginnovationhub.co with your event details and preferred date.
