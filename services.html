Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you take equity from incubated startups?
+
No — MG Innovation Hub does not take any equity from startups in our incubation program. We believe founders should retain full ownership of their companies. Our incubation fees are program-based and clearly outlined during the application process.
What stage should my startup be at to apply?
+
We work with founders from idea stage all the way to Series A. Most cohort members are pre-seed or seed stage — they have a clear problem, some early validation, and a team. We also have a "pre-incubation" track for solo founders still forming their team.
Can I attend workshops without a co-working membership?
+
Absolutely. Our bootcamps and workshops are open to the public. You don't need a membership to enrol — just register and pay the bootcamp fee. Existing members get a 20% discount on all workshops.
Is remote participation available?
+
For bootcamps, yes — approximately 60% of sessions are available remotely via our live-stream and recording platform. For the incubation program, we require physical presence for at least 70% of sessions, particularly the milestone reviews and Demo Day.
How do I know if I qualify for investor introductions?
+
Investor matchmaking is available to active incubation cohort members and Pro/Scale co-working members. Your account manager will assess your funding readiness and match you to appropriate investors based on your sector, stage, and raise size. We never make cold introductions — every intro is warm and pre-qualified on both sides.
Are there discounts for teams or NGOs?
+
Yes. Teams of 3 or more get 10% off co-working plans. Registered NGOs and social enterprises get 20% off all programs. Contact us with proof of registration to access these rates.
